Cereal Staple

Maize (Corn)

  • How to Grow: Plant certified hybrid seeds at onset of rains; spacing 75cm × 30cm, 2–3cm deep.
  • Improve Output: Apply DAP at planting and top-dress with CAN at knee-height. Keep weed-free in first 4 weeks.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Control Fall Armyworm early with targeted pesticides; prevent Maize Lethal Necrosis (MLN) via crop rotation.
Yield Target: 25–35 Bags/Acre Ask Expert →
Legume & Nitrogen Fixer

Beans

  • How to Grow: Sow in well-drained loamy soil at 45cm × 15cm spacing. Avoid waterlogged fields.
  • Improve Output: Inoculate seeds with Rhizobium and apply phosphatic fertilizer (TSP/DAP) for heavy podding.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Use certified disease-free seeds to prevent Anthracnose & Bean Rust; spray neem or copper fungicides early.
Harvest: 75–90 Days Ask Expert →
Leafy Vegetable

Sukuma Wiki (Collard Greens)

  • How to Grow: Raise seedlings in a nursery for 3–4 weeks, then transplant at 60cm × 45cm with well-rotted manure.
  • Improve Output: Top-dress with CAN or foliar nitrogen every 3 weeks; harvest lower leaves weekly to stimulate new growth.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Rotate with non-brassicas to stop Black Rot & Clubroot; control Aphids & Diamondback Moth promptly.
Continuous Weekly Harvest Ask Expert →
High-Value Greens

Spinach

  • How to Grow: Transplant healthy seedlings at 30cm × 30cm in rich, moisture-retentive soil (pH 6.0–6.8).
  • Improve Output: Use drip irrigation and organic compost mulch to produce broad, tender, dark-green leaves.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Avoid overhead watering at night to prevent Downy Mildew & Leaf Spot; scout for leaf miners.
First Pick: 35–45 Days Ask Expert →
Perennial Fruit Crop

Banana

  • How to Grow: Dig 2ft × 2ft × 2ft holes spaced 3m × 3m; mix topsoil with 20kg manure and plant tissue-culture suckers.
  • Improve Output: Maintain 3 stems per stool (mother, daughter, granddaughter), mulch heavily, and apply potassium-rich fertilizer.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Use clean tissue-culture plantlets to stop Panama Disease & Banana Weevils; prune dry leaves.
Bunch Weight: 30–50 kg Ask Expert →
Export Cash Crop

Coffee

  • How to Grow: Plant disease-resistant varieties (Ruiru 11 / Batian) in deep volcanic soils at 2m × 2m spacing with shade trees.
  • Improve Output: Practice annual pruning to open the canopy, apply NPK 17:17:17, and add magnesium/boron foliar feeds.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Spray copper-based fungicides before rains to prevent Coffee Berry Disease (CBD) & Coffee Leaf Rust.
Peak Cherry Quality Ask Expert →
Highland Cash Crop

Tea

  • How to Grow: Plant high-yielding clones in acidic soils (pH 4.5–5.5) at 1.2m × 0.75m spacing in cool, high-rainfall areas.
  • Improve Output: Pluck strictly "two leaves and a bud" every 7–10 days; apply NPK 26:5:5 fertilizer and prune every 3–4 years.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Maintain good drainage to prevent Armillaria Root Rot; control Red Spider Mites during dry spells.
Pluck Cycle: 7–10 Days Ask Expert →
High-Return Horticulture

Tomato

  • How to Grow: Transplant sturdy seedlings at 60cm × 45cm; stake/trellis plants early to keep fruits off the soil.
  • Improve Output: Prune side suckers, irrigate consistently, and apply calcium-boron foliar sprays to prevent Blossom End Rot.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Alternate Mancozeb/Metalaxyl against Early & Late Blight; use pheromone traps for Tuta Absoluta.
Harvest: 70–85 Days Ask Expert →
Large-Scale Poultry

Hens (Layers & Broilers)

  • Housing & Setup: Well-ventilated deep-litter or battery cage house with dry wood shavings, footbaths, and 2 sq.ft per bird.
  • Improve Output: Feed stage-specific rations (Chick Mash → Growers → Layers/Finisher), clean water 24/7, and 16 hrs light for layers.
  • Disease Control: Follow strict vaccination schedule (Newcastle, Gumboro, Fowl Typhoid & Fowl Pox) and strict biosecurity.
Layers: 85–92% Egg Rate Vet Support →
Commercial Dairy & Beef

Cows (Zero-Grazing & Herd)

  • Housing & Setup: Spacious cubicles with rubber mats, proper slope drainage, clean feed troughs, and constant water access.
  • Improve Output: Total Mixed Ration (Napier/Boma Rhodes silage + Dairy Meal based on milk yield + high-phosphorus mineral licks).
  • Disease Control: Weekly acaricide spraying against East Coast Fever (ticks), teat dipping for Mastitis, and FMD vaccinations.
Target: 20–35 Liters/Day Vet Support →
High-Turnover Piggery

Pigs (Farrow-to-Finish)

  • Housing & Setup: Concrete-floored pens with farrowing rails for piglets, shaded sleeping area, and automatic nipple drinkers.
  • Improve Output: Feed balanced Creep, Weaner, Grower, and Finisher meals; reach 90kg live weight in 5.5–6 months.
  • Disease Control: Strict quarantine & disinfectant footbaths against African Swine Fever (ASF); deworm every 3 months & give iron to piglets.
Market Weight: 5.5 Months Vet Support →
Small Ruminants Herd

Goats & Sheep (Large Flocks)

  • Housing & Setup: Raised slatted wooden floors (4ft off ground) to keep hooves dry and prevent parasite buildup.
  • Improve Output: Supplement pasture/hay with Calliandra/Lucerne fodder, concentrates, and mineral blocks for fast twinning & weight gain.
  • Disease Control: Vaccinate against CCPP & PPR; trim hooves regularly to prevent Foot Rot and deworm rotationally.
High Twinning & Dairy Yield Vet Support →
Commercial Aquaculture

Fish Farming (Tilapia & Catfish)

  • Pond Setup: Liner ponds or cages stocked with monosex (all-male) fingerlings at 3–5 fish per square meter.
  • Improve Output: Feed high-protein floating pellets (30–35% CP) twice daily at fixed feeding corners and aerate water.
  • Disease Control: Monitor dissolved oxygen, pH, and ammonia; prevent overcrowding and fungal infections via clean water exchange.
Harvest: 6–8 Months (500g+) Vet Support →
