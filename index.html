Cereal Staple

Maize (Corn)

  • How to Grow: Plant certified hybrid seeds at onset of rains; spacing 75cm × 30cm, 2–3cm deep.
  • Improve Output: Apply DAP at planting and top-dress with CAN at knee-height. Keep weed-free in first 4 weeks.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Control Fall Armyworm early with targeted pesticides; prevent Maize Lethal Necrosis (MLN) via crop rotation.
Yield Target: 25–35 Bags/Acre Crop Expert →
🫘
Legume & Nitrogen Fixer

Beans

  • How to Grow: Sow in well-drained loamy soil at 45cm × 15cm spacing. Avoid waterlogged fields.
  • Improve Output: Inoculate seeds with Rhizobium and apply phosphatic fertilizer (TSP/DAP) for heavy podding.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Use certified disease-free seeds to prevent Anthracnose & Bean Rust; spray neem or copper fungicides early.
Harvest: 75–90 Days Crop Expert →
🥬
Leafy Vegetable

Sukuma Wiki (Collard Greens)

  • How to Grow: Raise seedlings in a nursery for 3–4 weeks, then transplant at 60cm × 45cm with well-rotted manure.
  • Improve Output: Top-dress with CAN or foliar nitrogen every 3 weeks; harvest lower leaves weekly to stimulate new growth.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Rotate with non-brassicas to stop Black Rot & Clubroot; control Aphids & Diamondback Moth promptly.
Continuous Weekly Harvest Crop Expert →
🍃
High-Value Greens

Spinach

  • How to Grow: Transplant healthy seedlings at 30cm × 30cm in rich, moisture-retentive soil (pH 6.0–6.8).
  • Improve Output: Use drip irrigation and organic compost mulch to produce broad, tender, dark-green leaves.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Avoid overhead watering at night to prevent Downy Mildew & Leaf Spot; scout for leaf miners.
First Pick: 35–45 Days Crop Expert →
🍌
Perennial Fruit Crop

Banana

  • How to Grow: Dig 2ft × 2ft × 2ft holes spaced 3m × 3m; mix topsoil with 20kg manure and plant tissue-culture suckers.
  • Improve Output: Maintain 3 stems per stool (mother, daughter, granddaughter), mulch heavily, and apply potassium-rich fertilizer.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Use clean tissue-culture plantlets to stop Panama Disease & Banana Weevils; prune dry leaves.
Bunch Weight: 30–50 kg Crop Expert →

Export Cash Crop

Coffee Plant & Cherries

  • How to Grow: Plant disease-resistant varieties (Ruiru 11 / Batian) in deep volcanic soils at 2m × 2m spacing with shade trees.
  • Improve Output: Practice annual pruning to open the canopy, apply NPK 17:17:17, and add magnesium/boron foliar feeds.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Spray copper-based fungicides before rains to prevent Coffee Berry Disease (CBD) & Coffee Leaf Rust.
Peak Cherry Quality Crop Expert →
🍵
Highland Cash Crop

Tea Plant (Unprocessed)

  • How to Grow: Plant high-yielding clones in acidic soils (pH 4.5–5.5) at 1.2m × 0.75m spacing in cool, high-rainfall areas.
  • Improve Output: Pluck strictly "two leaves and a bud" every 7–10 days; apply NPK 26:5:5 fertilizer and prune every 3–4 years.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Maintain good drainage to prevent Armillaria Root Rot; control Red Spider Mites during dry spells.
Pluck Cycle: 7–10 Days Crop Expert →
🍅
High-Return Horticulture

Tomato

  • How to Grow: Transplant sturdy seedlings at 60cm × 45cm; stake/trellis plants early to keep fruits off the soil.
  • Improve Output: Prune side suckers, irrigate consistently, and apply calcium-boron foliar sprays to prevent Blossom End Rot.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Alternate Mancozeb/Metalaxyl against Early & Late Blight; use pheromone traps for Tuta Absoluta.
Harvest: 70–85 Days Crop Expert →
🥔
Staple Tuber

Irish Potatoes

  • How to Grow: Plant certified sprouted seed tubers 10–15cm deep in ridges spaced 75cm × 30cm in cool, well-drained soils.
  • Improve Output: Earth up plants at 4–6 weeks to cover tubers, apply manure and NPK 7:14:7 fertilizer at planting.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Spray preventive fungicides against Late Blight; practice 3-year crop rotation to stop Bacterial Wilt.
Maturity: 90–110 Days Crop Expert →
🎋
Industrial Cash Crop

Sugarcane Plant

  • How to Grow: Plant healthy cane setts with 2–3 active buds in furrows 1.2m apart during the onset of long rains.
  • Improve Output: Weed regularly until canopy closes, apply nitrogen fertilizers (Urea/CAN), and trash-mulch to retain moisture.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Control stalk borers with integrated pest management; plant disease-free certified setts to prevent Ratoon Stunting.
Maturity: 12–18 Months Crop Expert →
🌾
Drought-Resistant Cereal

Sorghum

  • How to Grow: Drill seeds in rows spaced 60cm apart at 2–3cm depth in semi-arid or medium-potential areas.
  • Improve Output: Thin seedlings to 15cm apart within rows; apply phosphate fertilizer at planting for strong root establishment.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Use bird scarers or nets during grain filling; treat seeds with fungicides to prevent Smut and mold.
Drought Tolerant Crop Expert →
🍚
Paddy Cereal

Rice

  • How to Grow: Transplant 21-day nursery seedlings into leveled, puddled, and flooded paddies with 20cm × 20cm spacing.
  • Improve Output: Maintain controlled water levels (5–10cm) during vegetative stage; apply nitrogen in split doses.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Manage water drainage to control Rice Blast and stem borers; use certified seeds.
Paddy Irrigation Hub Crop Expert →
🌾
Nutritious Grain

Millet

  • How to Grow: Broadcast or drill seeds in shallow furrows (1–2cm deep) during early rains in well-drained sandy loams.
  • Improve Output: Thin out overcrowded seedlings and weed during the first 3 weeks to prevent choking.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Protect ripening heads from birds; use disease-free seeds to prevent Downy Mildew.
Harvest: 60–90 Days Crop Expert →
🍠
Nutritious Tuber

Sweet Potatoes

  • How to Grow: Plant disease-free vine cuttings (30cm long) on mounds or ridges at an angle, burying 2–3 nodes.
  • Improve Output: Keep mounds weed-free until vines cover the ground; avoid excessive nitrogen which causes lush leaves over tubers.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Select clean planting vines to prevent Sweet Potato Virus Disease (SPVD); monitor for sweet potato weevils.
Maturity: 3–5 Months Crop Expert →
🌿
Resilient Root Crop

Cassava Plant & Tubers

  • How to Grow: Plant mature stem cuttings (20–25cm) horizontally or slantwise on ridges spaced 1m × 1m.
  • Improve Output: Weed during early growth stages (0–3 months); harvest selectively or all at once when mature.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Use mosaic-resistant varieties to prevent Cassava Mosaic Disease (CMD); control green mites with bio-controls.
Drought Hardy Crop Expert →
☁️
Fiber Cash Crop

Cotton Plant & Bolls

  • How to Grow: Sow 3–4 seeds per hole at 90cm × 30cm spacing in warm, fertile, well-drained black cotton or loam soils.
  • Improve Output: Thin to 2 plants per hill after 3 weeks, apply NPK fertilizer, and ensure adequate sunshine.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Scout closely for bollworms and stainers; apply recommended insecticides during flowering and boll formation.
High Quality Lint Crop Expert →
🌵
Fiber & Hard Crop

Sisal Plant

  • How to Grow: Plant healthy bulbils or nursery sucker plants in rows spaced 3m to 4m apart in dry, well-aerated soils.
  • Improve Output: Keep fields free of weeds while young; harvest mature leaves starting at year 3 without damaging heart leaves.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Prevent Sisal Weevils by maintaining clean field borders and removing rotting plant debris.
Drought Resistant Crop Expert →
🌼
Natural Insecticide Crop

Pyrethrum Flowers

  • How to Grow: Transplant seedlings raised in nurseries into high-altitude, cool, well-drained soils at 60cm × 30cm spacing.
  • Improve Output: Apply phosphatic fertilizers at planting; pick open flower heads regularly to stimulate continuous flowering.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Avoid waterlogging to prevent Root Rot; control thrips and red spider mites using safe acaricides.
Highland Cash Crop Crop Expert →
🍃
Commercial Leaf Crop

Tobacco Plant

  • How to Grow: Transplant delicate seedlings from float trays or seedbeds into ridges spaced 100cm × 50cm in sandy loam soils.
  • Improve Output: Top plants (remove flowers) to redirect energy into broad leaves; sucker regularly.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Practice rotation with non-host crops to prevent Black Shank and Root-Knot Nematodes.
Cured Leaf Yield Crop Expert →

Wetland Tuber

Arrowroots (Nduma)

  • How to Grow: Plant rhizomes or suckers in wet, marshy riverbanks or trenches with constant moisture at 60cm × 45cm spacing.
  • Improve Output: Apply well-rotted compost manure and maintain high soil saturation or irrigation water flow.
  • Fight Diseases & Pests: Keep water channels clean to prevent root rot and fungal infections; weed during early stages.
High Moisture Need Crop Expert →

Large-Scale Poultry

Hens (Layers & Broilers)

  • Housing & Setup: Well-ventilated deep-litter or battery cage house with dry wood shavings, footbaths, and 2 sq.ft per bird.
  • Improve Output: Feed stage-specific rations (Chick Mash → Growers → Layers/Finisher), clean water 24/7, and 16 hrs light for layers.
  • Disease Control: Follow strict vaccination schedule (Newcastle, Gumboro, Fowl Typhoid & Fowl Pox) and strict biosecurity.
Layers: 85–92% Egg Rate Vet Support →
🐄
Commercial Dairy & Beef

Cows (Zero-Grazing & Herd)

  • Housing & Setup: Spacious cubicles with rubber mats, proper slope drainage, clean feed troughs, and constant water access.
  • Improve Output: Total Mixed Ration (Napier/Boma Rhodes silage + Dairy Meal based on milk yield + high-phosphorus mineral licks).
  • Disease Control: Weekly acaricide spraying against East Coast Fever (ticks), teat dipping for Mastitis, and FMD vaccinations.
Target: 20–35 Liters/Day Vet Support →
🐖
High-Turnover Piggery

Pigs (Farrow-to-Finish)

  • Housing & Setup: Concrete-floored pens with farrowing rails for piglets, shaded sleeping area, and automatic nipple drinkers.
  • Improve Output: Feed balanced Creep, Weaner, Grower, and Finisher meals; reach 90kg live weight in 5.5–6 months.
  • Disease Control: Strict quarantine & disinfectant footbaths against African Swine Fever (ASF); deworm every 3 months & give iron to piglets.
Market Weight: 5.5 Months Vet Support →
🐐
Small Ruminants Herd

Goats & Sheep (Large Flocks)

  • Housing & Setup: Raised slatted wooden floors (4ft off ground) to keep hooves dry and prevent parasite buildup.
  • Improve Output: Supplement pasture/hay with Calliandra/Lucerne fodder, concentrates, and mineral blocks for fast twinning & weight gain.
  • Disease Control: Vaccinate against CCPP & PPR; trim hooves regularly to prevent Foot Rot and deworm rotationally.
High Twinning & Dairy Yield Vet Support →
🐟
Commercial Aquaculture

Fish Farming (Tilapia in Pond)

  • Pond Setup: Liner ponds or cages stocked with monosex (all-male) fingerlings at 3–5 fish per square meter.
  • Improve Output: Feed high-protein floating pellets (30–35% CP) twice daily at fixed feeding corners and aerate water.
  • Disease Control: Monitor dissolved oxygen, pH, and ammonia; prevent overcrowding and fungal infections via clean water exchange.

Fast Breeding Livestock

Rabbits

  • Housing & Setup: Well-ventilated hutches with wire-mesh floors for droppings clearance, nest boxes, and fresh drinking bottles.
  • Improve Output: Feed clean wilted lucerne, desmodium, rabbit pellets, and clean hay; keep breeding records strictly.
  • Disease Control: Prevent Viral Hemorrhagic Disease (VHD) through annual vaccination; keep cages dry to prevent mange and ear mites.
Fast Maturity (4 Months) Vet Support →
🐪
Arid Land Herd

Camels

  • Housing & Setup: Large open bomas with sturdy fencing in arid/semi-arid rangelands with natural shade trees.
  • Improve Output: Browser grazing on acacia, shrubs, and supplemental salt licks for high-protein camel milk production.
  • Disease Control: Treat regularly against trypanosomiasis (surra), mange mites, and tick-borne diseases.
High-Value Camel Milk Vet Support →
🫏
Farm Draft & Transport

Donkeys

  • Housing & Setup: Dry, draft-free shelters with access to clean water and roughage forage.
  • Improve Output: Provide regular hoof trimming, balanced forage, and adequate rest periods from heavy cart pulling.
  • Disease Control: Routine deworming, vaccination against tetanus, and prompt wound care.
Strong Farm Helper Vet Support →
🐝
Apiary & Honey

Bees (Swarm / Apiculture)

  • Housing & Setup: Langstroth or Kenya Top Bar Hives installed in quiet, shaded, secure apiaries near flowering flora.
  • Improve Output: Provide clean water sources nearby; maintain strong colonies with productive queen bees.
  • Disease Control: Protect hives from honey badgers, ants, and small hive beetles with guarded stands and clean smoker management.
High Honey & Wax Yield Vet Support →
🦃
Large Poultry Enterprise

Turkeys

  • Housing & Setup: Spacious predator-proof runs and dry shelters with high perches. Poults require extra warmth in first 6 weeks.
  • Improve Output: Feed high-protein turkey starter and grower rations mixed with fresh greens.
  • Disease Control: Prevent Blackhead disease by not raising turkeys on land previously used by chickens; vaccinate against fowl pox.
